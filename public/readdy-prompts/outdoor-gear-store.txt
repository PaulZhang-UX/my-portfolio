============================================================
1. 设计基础 Prompt
============================================================

Create a premium quiet-technical outdoor gear website for a brand called NORTHVALE. Use a dark charcoal narrative shell, warm off-white and white commerce surfaces, and a restrained burnt-orange primary action color. The visual language should feel field-tested, editorial, alpine, durable, and calm rather than glossy, loud, or promotional.

Use Inter as the primary sans-serif for navigation, product information, controls, and most headings. Use a limited italic editorial serif similar to Fraunces only for poetic hero or field-note phrases. Use uppercase micro-labels with generous letter spacing around 0.20em to 0.24em. Keep headings light to regular weight, with compact line-height and generous surrounding space.

Build the layout around cinematic full-bleed outdoor photography, wide horizontal margins, large image-led sections, and a restrained 2-column mobile / 4-column desktop product grid. Use neutral thin borders, very limited shadows, low-radius or square surfaces, and strong alignment. Product imagery should be contained in light neutral containers; editorial and hero imagery should use object-cover with deliberate crops.

Use muted alpine, forest, stone, charcoal, sand, clay, moss, and terracotta tones. Primary buttons and important action states may use warm burnt orange. Avoid loud gradients, glossy product-card effects, oversized rounded cards, heavy drop shadows, generic stock-adventure imagery, and dense promotional badges.

Animations should be slow and tactile: subtle image scale on hover, decode-before-display image crossfades, short page-enter fades, smooth drawer transitions, and a restrained pointer-driven weather-glass effect in the main hero. Respect prefers-reduced-motion and keep content readable and stable while imagery loads.

============================================================
2. 整站重建 Prompt
============================================================

Project Definition

Reconstruct a complete responsive front-end website for NORTHVALE, a quiet technical outdoor gear brand. The site sells field-tested outerwear, tops, bottoms, trail footwear, packs, and accessories, while presenting material stories, field notes, routes, and brand philosophy. This is a front-end template with static product and editorial data. Do not invent a backend, database, account system, payment integration, or CMS.

Global Visual Direction

Create an editorial alpine outdoor aesthetic that feels calm, durable, technical, and field-tested. Combine a dark charcoal brand narrative shell with white and warm off-white commerce surfaces. Use muted forest, stone, moss, sand, clay, slate, and charcoal tones, with restrained burnt orange for primary actions. The interface should feel premium through typography, image scale, spacing, and material storytelling rather than gradients, heavy shadows, sale badges, or dense cards.

Use cinematic outdoor photography: alpine ridges, wet rock, quiet trails, technical fabric, seams, packs, weather, and muted natural light. Avoid glossy fashion campaigns, neon sports colors, generic stock-adventure poses, loud sale labels, and decorative clutter.

Design System

Use Inter as the primary sans-serif. Use a limited italic editorial serif similar to Fraunces for poetic hero phrases and field-note accents. Use uppercase micro-labels with approximately 0.20em to 0.24em tracking. Prefer light to regular heading weights and compact line-height.

Use these verified visual directions:
- Dark narrative background around #1B1B1B.
- Footer dark around #111211.
- Light technical surface around #F1F1EE.
- White commerce sections.
- Warm orange primary action around #C26E1F.
- Product swatches include Charcoal #2A2A2A, Slate #4A5359, Moss #3D4A3A, Sand #C9B48F, Clay #B0623A, Black #1B1B1B, and Terracotta #B5533A.

Use 72px mobile and 80px desktop header heights. Use 20px mobile and 40px desktop horizontal page padding in standard sections, with a commerce grid max width around 1600px. Use 2 product columns on mobile and 4 on large desktop. Keep borders thin and neutral, corner radii restrained, and shadows minimal.

Use subtle motion: 220ms page enter with opacity and a 4px upward movement using cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1); around 900ms product image hover scale; around 1400ms category image hover scale; around 300ms menu and drawer transitions; decode-before-display image crossfades; and reduced-motion behavior that removes continuous effects.

Website Information Architecture

Implement these routes:
- / — home
- /shop — all products with URL-driven filters
- /shop/:slug — category landing and product grid
- /product/:slug — product detail
- /cart — full bag page
- /checkout — placeholder page stating that secure checkout is coming soon
- /technology — material and construction stories
- /our-story — brand philosophy and field testing
- /journal — editorial listing
- /journal/:slug — editorial detail
- /contact — contact form and support information
- * — not found

Do not implement /account as a real page unless explicitly requested. The original header contains an account icon, but the source route is missing and the click currently reaches the not-found page.

Global Application Shell

Create an application shell with a fixed NORTHVALE header, route transitions, a footer, a client-side cart provider, a cart drawer, and scroll restoration. The header has two variants:
- overlay: transparent over Home, Technology, Our Story, and Journal detail until the user scrolls more than roughly 40px, then dark translucent background with backdrop blur and a bottom border;
- solid: dark/solid header for Shop, category, product, cart, checkout, Journal listing, and Contact.

Use an uppercase NORTHVALE wordmark with approximately 0.24em letter spacing. On desktop show Shop, Technology, Our Story, Journal, and Contact. Show search linking to /shop, an account icon linking to /account, and a bag button that opens the cart drawer and displays item count. On mobile provide a menu button with the same links and bag count. Close the mobile menu when the pathname changes.

The footer uses a dark surface. Include a short brand description, a monthly Field Notes email form, and four link groups: Shop, Explore, Support, and Social. The newsletter form has a required email, a hidden honeypot field, loading/success/error states, and the success message “Thanks — you're on the list.”. Treat external form posting as an integration placeholder, not an internal backend.

Interaction and Technical Requirements

Implement overlay header scroll state, mobile menu close-on-pathname-change, body scroll lock for the drawer, SmartImage preload/decode/crossfade/fallback, one-time HeroLoader, WeatherGlass pointer-path mask and refog, page-enter transition, PUSH/POP scroll restoration, native accessible controls, alt text, keyboard focus, and reduced-motion behavior.

Use React, TypeScript, React Router, Tailwind CSS, and semantic HTML. Keep product and story data static and typed. Do not add a database, authentication, account dashboard, payment processor, inventory API, CMS, fake reviews, or fake order confirmation. If WebGL2 is unavailable, use a readable static hero fallback and document the limitation.

Quality Assurance

Verify every route, exact Home order, category links, featured product slugs, product filters, audience logic, swatch state, cart merge/localStorage behavior, totals, drawer close, menu close, invalid redirects, form states, image fallback, reduced motion, and the /account not-found limitation. Check narrow, medium, and wide layouts. Check that remote image failure does not collapse layout and that the WebGL canvas does not block text or controls. Preserve source-confirmed labels, route boundaries, interactions, and data relationships.
