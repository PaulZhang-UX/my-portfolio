1. 设计基础 Prompt
============================================================

Create a warm, editorial residential design-build website for a studio named Atelier Ridge. The visual language should feel quiet, architectural, tactile, and highly considered. Use a restrained palette of white (#FFFFFF), warm beige surfaces (#E7E0D6 and #EDE6DC), deep brown-black ink (#1D1A17), secondary ink (#5A544C), and dark charcoal sections (#1A1A1A). Avoid saturated brand colors, heavy shadows, glossy gradients, and generic SaaS styling.

Use Inter for body text, navigation, forms, metadata, and uppercase eyebrow labels. Use Cormorant Garamond for display headings and the editorial wordmark, with a refined serif contrast and generous whitespace. Eyebrow labels should be small, uppercase, semi-bold, and visibly tracked. Keep headings large, calm, and tightly composed rather than loud.

Build a 1200px content system inside a 1440px page canvas. Use generous vertical spacing, thin hairline borders, strict image ratios, and a mixture of two-column, three-column, and twelve-column layouts. Use 16:9 images for hero/detail storytelling and 4:3 images for project cards and editorial frames. Let selected images bleed to the viewport edge while keeping text aligned to a disciplined content grid.

Use understated controls: text links, thin bordered buttons, and arrow links with a small horizontal arrow movement on hover. Use a fixed navigation bar that is transparent over the home hero and becomes white with a subtle bottom border after scrolling. Use a dark, spacious footer with the studio wordmark and concise navigation.

The motion language should be slow, soft, and spatial: opacity and small vertical reveals, editorial image slides, a pointer-driven before/after image mask, and scroll-driven image framing. Use cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1) for major entrances, keep hover scaling subtle, pause continuous testimonial motion on hover, and honor prefers-reduced-motion by making content immediately visible and removing nonessential transforms.

Use authentic residential interiors, natural materials, custom joinery, stone, hardware, and construction details as the image direction. Favor warm natural light, low saturation, honest textures, and magazine-quality architectural composition. Preserve the reading order and image ratios on mobile, replacing complex desktop pinned scenes with simple stacked content when necessary.
