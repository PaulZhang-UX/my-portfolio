1. 设计基础 Prompt
============================================================

Create a warm, modern neighborhood veterinary clinic design system for a brand called Alder Veterinary Clinic.

Brand character:
- Calm, caring, unhurried, trustworthy, community-oriented, and editorial rather than corporate.
- Communicate low-stress veterinary care for pets and the people who love them.
- The visual language should feel like a thoughtful local clinic with strong medical standards, not like a generic animal hospital or a cold medical dashboard.

Color system:
- Use white #FFFFFF as the primary surface.
- Use warm cream #FFF9F2 for alternating sections, cards, and the footer.
- Use butter yellow #F2D16B for hero backgrounds, approach sections, closing CTAs, badges, and numbered markers.
- Use soft yellow #F8E4A8, pale peach #FBEBD6, and pale cream #FFF3E4 for large, quiet organic background blobs.
- Use deep cocoa brown #5C3A21 for headings, logos, icons, and primary buttons.
- Use darker cocoa #3E2614 for button hover states.
- Use near-black warm ink #1F1B17 for body text and muted brown #8A7467 for secondary labels.
- Use warm beige borders such as #EDE5DA and #F1EBE3.
- Keep contrast readable and avoid introducing clinical blue, neon green, or harsh black unless required for accessibility.

Typography:
- Use Fraunces as the expressive display serif for all major headings, editorial statements, numbers, and brand moments.
- Use DM Sans for navigation, body copy, labels, forms, buttons, and supporting information.
- Use large, confident headlines with tight line-height around 0.98–1.05.
- Use uppercase eyebrow labels at 12–13px with semibold weight and generous letter spacing around 0.14–0.18em.
- Use 15–18px body copy with comfortable relaxed line-height.

Layout and surfaces:
- Build within a centered content container around 1320px wide with 20px mobile padding and 48px desktop padding.
- Use generous vertical rhythm, usually 96px on mobile and 128px on desktop for major sections.
- Prefer 12-column editorial grids on desktop and clean single-column stacks on mobile.
- Use large 28–36px corner radii for cards and image containers.
- Use full pill buttons around 52–54px tall.
- Alternate white and warm cream backgrounds; reserve butter yellow for high-emphasis narrative moments.

Component language:
- Primary buttons are cocoa pill buttons with white text, a right arrow icon, and a gentle lift on hover.
- Secondary actions are underlined or arrowed cocoa text links.
- Cards use quiet warm backgrounds, low-contrast borders, large corners, and restrained hover lifts.
- Form fields use cream backgrounds, rounded pill shapes, cocoa-tinted borders, and a calm focus ring.
- Use paw, heart, calendar, time, phone, location, and medical utility icons with a consistent thin/solid icon language.

Image direction:
- Use bright editorial photography of veterinarians, dogs, cats, owners, and warm modern clinic spaces.
- Prefer natural daylight, warm ivory walls, subtle olive or sage accents, authentic human-animal interaction, and calm clinical moments.
- Avoid dark dramatic lighting, generic stock-photo poses, glossy hospital aesthetics, and overly saturated colors.
- Crop images with object-cover and use a small family of asymmetric organic border-radius shapes instead of only rectangles.

Motion language:
- Use cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1) for soft, responsive easing.
- Keep micro-interactions around 200–380ms.
- Use 600–650ms transitions for Hero image swaps and organic shape changes.
- Use 320ms accordion transitions with a subtle upward settle.
- Let decorative blobs rotate or drift very slowly over 32–55 seconds.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion and disable custom motion where appropriate.

Responsive behavior:
- Keep the editorial hierarchy intact across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Collapse desktop grids into a single stack on mobile.
- Use a desktop navigation with anchor links and a mobile menu panel.
- Keep image crops expressive but prevent faces, pets, and important clinical details from being cut off.
- Hide or simplify large decorative blobs on narrow screens.
- Ensure forms remain one column and touch targets stay at least about 44–54px high.

2. 整站重建 Prompt
============================================================

Reconstruct a complete responsive veterinary clinic marketing website called Alder Veterinary Clinic, using the following requirements as implementation truth.

Project Definition

Build a warm, modern neighborhood veterinary clinic website for pets and the people who love them. The site should communicate calm, low-stress, thoughtful medical care, clear communication, and a modern general-practice offering. It must feel editorial and human, not like a cold hospital dashboard or a generic pet store.

Use React, TypeScript, Vite, React Router, and Tailwind CSS or an equivalent component system. Implement a real multi-page route structure with shared navigation, footer, route transitions, anchor navigation, responsive layouts, and accessible form controls.

Global Visual Direction

- Use a warm editorial visual direction with white, cream, butter yellow, and deep cocoa brown.
- Primary surfaces: white #FFFFFF and warm cream #FFF9F2.
- High-emphasis sections: butter yellow #F2D16B.
- Deep cocoa brand color: #5C3A21; dark hover cocoa: #3E2614.
- Body ink: #1F1B17; muted brown: #8A7467; warm borders: #EDE5DA.
- Use large organic blobs in #F8E4A8, #FBEBD6, and #FFF3E4 as quiet background decoration.
- Use Fraunces for display headings and DM Sans for body/UI copy.
- Keep the tone calm, specific, trustworthy, and unhurried.
- Use bright natural editorial photography of veterinarians, dogs, cats, owners, and warm clinic spaces. Do not use dark dramatic lighting or generic corporate stock imagery.

Design System

- Center all content in a 1320px maximum-width container.
- Use 20px horizontal padding on mobile and 48px horizontal padding on medium/desktop screens.
- Use 96px to 128px vertical padding for major sections.
- Use 12-column desktop grids, two-column tablet patterns where appropriate, and single-column mobile stacks.
- Use 28px, 32px, or 36px radius values for large cards and image containers.
- Use full-pill primary buttons around 52–54px high, with right-arrow icons.
- Primary buttons use cocoa background and white text; hover to #3E2614 and lift slightly.
- Secondary links use cocoa text with an underline or arrow; arrow icons move about 5px right on hover.
- Cards have quiet warm backgrounds, low-contrast borders, large radii, and restrained lift on hover.
- Use organic asymmetric border-radius shapes for selected images. Keep the family of shapes consistent across the site.
- Use a 200–380ms soft easing for micro interactions and a 600–650ms soft easing for Hero image/shape changes.
- Honor prefers-reduced-motion for CSS and JavaScript motion.

Website Information Architecture

Implement these routes:

1. `/` — Home.
2. `/services` — Services overview.
3. `/services/:slug` — Dynamic service detail page. Support the slugs `wellness-care`, `dental-care`, `diagnostics`, `surgery`, and `urgent-care`.
4. `/about` — Clinic approach, team, environment, and care standards.
5. `/book` — Appointment request form.
6. `*` — A friendly 404 page with a link back to Home.

The shared shell must contain a sticky 72px header, route-aware navigation, a footer, a light page-enter transition, manual scroll restoration on route changes, and a desktop-only paw cursor system. The desktop navigation links to Home anchors: Services, Our Approach, Team, and Visit Us. The header also contains a primary “Book an Appointment” button. On mobile, replace the links with a menu button and a vertical menu panel.
