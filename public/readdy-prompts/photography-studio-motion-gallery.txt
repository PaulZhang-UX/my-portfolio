============================================================

Create a full-screen editorial photography studio interface for a brand called Lumen Studio. The experience should feel quiet, tactile, art-directed, and premium, combining Scandinavian restraint with contemporary motion-gallery behavior.

Use a warm-to-cool theme system with four switchable color moods:
- Rose Clay: base #D96E6A, center #E28480, edge #B85956.
- Mineral: base #6088A1, center #7699AF, edge #4C7288.
- Iris: base #8385BE, center #9698CB, edge #6D6FA8.
- Ink: base #2C3138, center #3B4149, edge #1E2228.
Render each gallery background as a subtle radial gradient, not a flat fill. Use the theme base as the current accent color and the darker theme color for image-card backing.

Typography:
- Use Inter at weights 300, 400, and 500 for body copy and controls.
- Use Fraunces at heavy weight for the Lumen wordmark and large editorial headings, with tight negative tracking.
- Use small uppercase labels with generous letter spacing around 0.3em to 0.42em.
- Keep body text around 15px to 17px with line-height between 1.6 and 1.7.

Layout:
- The primary gallery is a fixed full-viewport canvas with hidden page overflow.
- Desktop cards are 240px wide by 360px tall with 48px gaps and 48px left inset.
- Mobile cards are 60vw wide by 46vh tall with 20px gaps and 20px left inset.
- Cards are fully pill-shaped with a 9999px radius and images use cover cropping centered on the subject.
- Use generous rounded corners everywhere: 30px to 48px for major image panels, 36px to 44px for information panels, and 64px for the content footer top corners.
- Use a white content world with #141210 ink, #8A827B muted text, and a very light rgba(20,18,16,0.09) border.

Component language:
- Buttons are compact rounded pills with uppercase micro-labels.
- Filters use translucent white frosted glass, a thin white border, blur, and a subtle inner highlight.
- Theme selectors are circular swatches with a larger outlined active state.
- Service rows are large rounded bands with thin borders and low-opacity accent hover fills.
- Forms are placed inside a softly tinted accent panel with white pill-shaped inputs.

Image direction:
- Use editorial portrait, documentary wedding, and quiet travel photography.
- Prefer medium-format film character, natural window light, muted neutral palettes, soft shadows, restrained expressions, and honest human moments.
- Keep the image treatment calm and tactile; avoid glossy stock-photo aesthetics and high-saturation commercial color.

Motion language:
- The gallery continuously drifts at a slow base speed and reacts to wheel, touch, and drag input with smooth acceleration and decay.
- Pause the gallery while a user hovers a work or reads a detail view.
- Preserve image ownership across hover, detail opening, and detail closing so the bitmap appears to travel continuously rather than disappear and reappear.
- Favor power2.out and power3.inOut easing, soft scale changes, subtle shadows, and a deliberate editorial rhythm.
- Respect the exact motion timings and trigger conditions described by the master reconstruction prompt.

Responsive behavior:
- Use a 767px mobile breakpoint.
- On mobile, replace desktop interaction hints with Swipe to explore, hide desktop center navigation, make detail content vertically scrollable, and make the detail CTA full width.
- Keep the brand, theme system, rounded image language, and calm motion identity consistent across breakpoints.

Project Definition

Reconstruct a complete front-end website for an independent editorial photography studio called Lumen Studio. The site is a full-screen motion gallery first, with a secondary white content world for the studio story, services, process, and contact inquiry. It is a front-end-only experience with no database, account system, cart, payment flow, or custom backend.

The primary route is / and should render the entire experience. A catch-all route may render a generic not-found screen. Do not create separate routes for Studio, Services, Contact, or individual works; those views are overlays or in-page worlds controlled by local UI state.

Global Visual Direction

Make the visual language quiet, tactile, editorial, and premium. Combine Scandinavian restraint, natural-light photography, medium-format film character, generous negative space, and contemporary motion design. The gallery must feel like a moving sequence of physical photographic prints rather than a conventional card grid.

Use a full-viewport colored gallery world with a subtle radial gradient. Use a separate white editorial content world when the user opens the studio information panel. Keep controls restrained so the photographs and the large Lumen wordmark remain the dominant visual elements.

Interaction and Motion Behavior

Gallery motion:
- Start the gallery at a baseline signed velocity of 60 pixels per second in the default right-to-left visual direction.
- Keep a signed accumulated position and wrap it by the measured width of one rendered copy.
- Write the track transform from one GSAP ticker only.
- Convert wheel, touch, and pointer drag input into a signed impulse. Use deltaY minus deltaX, normalize by 60, clamp each event to approximately plus or minus 4 units, multiply by a gain of 55, and clamp the accumulated impulse to plus or minus 260.
- Decay the impulse exponentially at 2.2 per second.
- Smooth the current velocity toward the baseline plus impulse at 6 per second. Cap both forward and reverse speed at 180 pixels per second.
- Clamp ticker delta time to a maximum of 0.1 seconds to survive tab-switch pauses.
- When hover, detail, or a content world requests a stop, smooth the velocity to zero, snap at an epsilon of 0.5, and stop writing position while fully stopped.
- When released, resume toward the baseline using gentler smoothing at 3.5 per second.

Hover behavior:
- On pointer enter, stop gallery motion.
- Clone the already-rendered source image into a fixed, pointer-events-none proxy layer so the browser can reuse the decoded bitmap.
- Preserve a fully pill-shaped border radius.
- Atomically reveal the proxy and hide the source. If the image is not decoded, wait for decode and commit the swap on the next animation frame so there is no blank frame.
- Scale the proxy to 1.05 and add a soft shadow over 0.4 seconds using power2.out.
- On pointer leave, animate the proxy back to the exact source item using Flip.fit over 0.35 seconds with power2.out, remove the shadow, reveal the source, remove the proxy, and resume gallery motion.
- If the pointer moves directly from one card to another, tear down the old proxy synchronously and establish the new proxy without restarting the gallery motion between cards.
- After returning from detail, suppress hover on the origin card until the pointer genuinely leaves it.

Detail opening:
- Allow activation from gallery or hover state only.
- If a hover proxy exists for the clicked card, inherit that proxy rather than creating a new image.
- Stop gallery motion, preload the selected image, and wait approximately 150ms before opening the detail state.
- Hide the source card and show the detail overlay.
- Use GSAP Flip.fit to move the proxy into the detail image box over 0.82 seconds with power3.inOut and a 24px final radius.
- Begin the detail text sequence around 0.5 seconds into the image transition.
- Animate category badge, title, description, metadata cards, CTA, and back action from opacity 0 and y 12 to visible using 0.55 seconds, power2.out, and 0.07 seconds stagger.
- Use a 2-second watchdog and restore the gallery safely if the transition cannot complete.

Detail closing:
- Only accept Back to gallery while the detail state is active.
- Stop gallery motion and set the origin card as the return destination.
- Clone the decoded detail image into a fixed proxy and animate top, left, width, height, and border radius back to the origin over 0.78 seconds using power3.inOut.
- Hide the detail image during the return.
- At completion, remeasure the current origin image, snap the proxy exactly over it, reveal the real card underneath, wait one animation frame, remove the proxy, restore gallery state, and resume motion with the gentler smoothing.
- If geometry or refs are invalid, run a safe teardown that restores both the detail image and the origin card.

Detail-to-services chain:
- When View services & pricing is clicked, first finish the detail close transition, wait for the next paint, open the content world, and target the Services section.
- Do not allow repeated chain activation while this sequence is in progress.

Content world:
- Opening translates the white surface upward with approximately 0.9 seconds and cubic-bezier(0.7, 0, 0.15, 1).
- Closing translates it down with approximately 0.75 seconds and the same easing.
- Keep a 26px top peek and a 132px-wide pull-tab. Animate a small arrow between up and down states.
- Add a 3.4-second infinite breathing animation to the closed pull-tab, moving it upward by 4px at the midpoint.
- When a top-nav section is requested, wait approximately 950ms for the panel to settle, then smooth-scroll to the section with a 72px sticky-header offset.
- Track the active section from the scroller position using a 140px reading threshold.
- The content world owns wheel/touch scrolling while open; the gallery Observer must be suspended.

Theme and filtering:
- The filter dropdown contains All, Portrait, Wedding, and Travel.
- Close the dropdown after selection and also when a mousedown happens outside its wrapper.
- The popover uses frosted glass, scale and opacity transitions, and an active dot.
- Theme swatches are Rose Clay, Mineral, Iris, and Ink. The active swatch is larger and outlined. Theme changes update the gallery background, item backing, white content-world accent, and detail overlay accent.

Technical Requirements

- Keep all content and form state local to the front end.
