1. 设计基础 Prompt
============================================================

Create a premium contemporary fine jewelry website for a brand called AURELIN. The visual language should feel quiet, architectural, warm, and carefully made in a small Antwerp atelier. Use a near-white canvas, pale stone surfaces, deep ink purple as the brand anchor, and muted brass as the accent. Use sale red-orange only for restrained sale and low-stock signals.

Use Inter for navigation, body copy, forms, labels, prices, and utility text. Use a light Cormorant Garamond-style serif for editorial headings, hero statements, and collection titles. Use uppercase eyebrow labels around 12px with approximately 0.16em letter spacing. Buttons should be rectangular, approximately 44px high, uppercase, and tracked around 0.20em; avoid rounded SaaS-style controls.

Build a centered 1320px content shell with 48px desktop, 32px tablet, and 20px mobile horizontal padding. Use spacious editorial sections, a 2-column mobile and 4-column desktop product grid, square or portrait jewelry imagery, and a 7/5 desktop product detail split. Keep borders thin, shadows very limited, and image backgrounds warm white or pale stone.

Use refined jewelry photography: white seamless packshots, close-up on-body portraits, warm neutral linen, stone, wood, brass tools, workshop hands, and soft natural window light. Emphasize recycled 18k gold, platinum, sterling silver, traceable stones, small runs, and hand finishing. Avoid neon colors, glossy over-retouching, loud luxury decoration, heavy cards, and generic stock photography.

Motion should be slow and tactile. Use a scroll-scrubbed muted jewelry film in the main hero, subtle image crossfades on product hover, restrained header and overlay transitions, a slow satin-like brand gradient, a paused-on-hover review marquee, animated accordions, and reduced-motion fallbacks. Preserve readable content and never require hover to access product information.

Project Definition

Reconstruct a complete responsive front-end ecommerce website for AURELIN, a contemporary fine jewelry house based in Antwerp. The catalog contains earrings, necklaces, rings, and bracelets made in recycled precious metals, platinum, sterling silver, and traceable stones. The brand should communicate quiet luxury, architectural simplicity, small-run production, hand finishing, and pieces intended for daily wear.

This is a static front-end template. Keep product and editorial content in typed local mock data. Do not invent a database, CMS, user account system, real payment processing, inventory API, or live customer service backend. External form POSTs and simulated chat replies must be documented as integration placeholders.

Global Visual Direction

Use a near-white canvas #FDFDFD, pale stone surface #F2F2F2, deep ink purple #1E144D, secondary satin purple #2A2061, muted brass #B2915B, graphite #434343, and restrained sale red-orange #BD4C2A. The mood is warm, quiet, precise, editorial, and architectural. Create luxury through proportion, material photography, type, and whitespace rather than gold gradients, ornate borders, heavy shadows, or promotional clutter.

Use refined contemporary jewelry imagery: white seamless packshots, close-up on-body crops, warm cream linen, neutral stone, wood workbenches, brass tools, soft window light, polishing, stone setting, chains, cuffs, rings, and small atelier scenes. Avoid neon, glossy fashion retouching, busy props, generic stock imagery, and loud luxury decoration.

Design System

Use Inter for body, navigation, labels, forms, prices, and utilities. Use a light Cormorant Garamond-style serif for display headings and editorial titles. Pacifico may remain unused. Eyebrow labels are uppercase, about 12–13px, weight 500, with approximately 0.16em letter spacing. Buttons are rectangular, about 44px high, uppercase, with approximately 0.20em tracking and no border radius.

Use a centered content shell with max width 1320px and horizontal padding 48px desktop, 32px tablet, and 20px mobile. Use white or pale stone image containers, thin neutral borders, low shadow, and 2-column mobile / 4-column desktop catalog grids. Use a 7/5 desktop gallery-to-purchase split on product pages.
