1. 设计基础 Prompt
============================================================

Create a modern green solar energy company website for a brand called Sunfield. Use a light cool-gray background, white content surfaces, deep near-black text, vivid solar green for primary actions and positive energy signals, and solar yellow for a small number of highlights, sun accents, and attention blocks. The visual direction should feel clean, architectural, reliable, data-informed, and calm rather than rustic, overly eco-themed, or corporate-heavy.

Use Inter for body copy and navigation, and a modern display sans similar to Instrument Sans for headings and large statistics. Use medium uppercase eyebrow labels with visible tracking. Use a 1440px content container with roughly 20px mobile, 40px tablet, 72px desktop, and 88px extra-wide horizontal padding. Use 12-column layouts, generous vertical spacing, rounded-2xl image containers, rounded 10px to 12px controls, thin cool-gray dividers, and clear 2-column or 3-column content grids.

Use realistic architectural photography showing black rooftop solar integrated into homes, offices, hospitality properties, and modern buildings. Pair large images with concise content, process steps, ROI numbers, energy impact metrics, financing choices, and a clear estimate form. Avoid generic green leaves, neon eco gradients, isolated panel product renders, and dense dashboard styling.

Use slow purposeful motion: a scroll-scrubbed solar community video hero, dynamic day-to-night text contrast, occasional subtle solar glints, intersection-triggered count-up statistics, staggered benefit reveals, a small project card tilt on pointer movement, and restrained route fades. Respect prefers-reduced-motion, keep the hero readable when video fails, and never hide essential actions behind hover.

2. 整站重建 Prompt
============================================================

Project Definition

Reconstruct a complete responsive front-end marketing website for Sunfield, a modern green solar energy company. Sunfield designs solar, storage, and energy management systems for homes, businesses, hospitality properties, and other real places. The site is a static front-end template with local content and image URLs. Do not invent a database, live energy calculator, account system, CRM, payment system, or backend quote engine.

Global Visual Direction

Make the site feel clean, architectural, modern, reliable, data-informed, and calm. Use a cool light-gray background, white surfaces, near-black ink text, vivid solar green for primary actions and positive energy signals, and solar yellow for carefully limited highlights. Use realistic modern architecture and rooftop solar photography, not generic environmental stock images.

Use Inter for body and navigation, and a modern display sans similar to Instrument Sans for headings and large statistics. Use uppercase eyebrow labels around 12px with tracking. Use a 1440px content shell, approximately 20px mobile, 40px tablet, 72px desktop, and 88px extra-wide horizontal padding. Use 12-column grids, generous vertical spacing, rounded-2xl image containers, rounded 10px to 12px controls, and thin cool-gray dividers.

Design System

Use:
- solar green #03D959 for primary buttons, positive signals, active progress, check icons, and brand mark;
- solar yellow #FFC905 for sun icons, glints, project accent block, financing highlight, and day/night cue;
- ink #111111 for headings and controls;
- muted #5F6368 for supporting text;
- background #F5F7FA;
- surface #FFFFFF;
- divider #E8EDF2;
- footer surface around #EDF1F5.

Buttons should be clear green rounded controls with dark text, around 48px to 52px high on major actions. Use soft rounded image containers and modest hover transitions. Avoid heavy shadows, neon gradients, leaves, excessive eco clichés, and dense analytics dashboards.

Application Shell and Routing

Implement these routes:
- / — Home
- /residential — Residential Solar
- /commercial — Commercial Solar
- /applications — Applications
- * — Not Found

Only render the full marketing Nav on /. Subpages use their own sticky header with a Back button and a green uppercase page label. Every subpage ends with the shared Sunfield Footer. Use a lightweight page fade on subpage mount, scroll the page to the top on mount, and honor reduced-motion.

Home Page Order

Build the Home page in this exact order:
1. Hero
2. Solutions
3. Benefits
4. Savings & ROI
5. Installation / Process
6. Projects
7. Financing
8. Energy Impact
9. Get Your Solar Estimate
10. Footer

Home Hero

Create an outer hero section approximately 560vh tall and a sticky inner stage approximately 100vh tall. Use a remote solar-community timelapse video as a muted, playsInline, preloaded, object-cover background with a static poster fallback. Do not treat it as a normal autoplay hero.

Place centered copy inside the stage:
- headline: “Clean energy for the way communities live.”
- supporting text: “Solar solutions for homes and businesses — bright by day, reliable by night.”
- primary CTA: “Get My Estimate”, scrolling smoothly to #estimate
- secondary CTA: “Explore Solutions”, scrolling smoothly to #solutions

Map the portion of the hero that has scrolled into view to a normalized target progress. Continuously chase the target video time with requestAnimationFrame. When target time is ahead, use a dynamic playbackRate clamped between approximately 1 and 12 to catch up; when target time is behind, pause and seek backwards in steps no larger than about 0.5 seconds per frame. Pause when the current time is within about 0.05 seconds of the target. Prime the first decoded frame from loadedmetadata and loadeddata/canplay events.

Change the title and supporting copy from dark text to light text as progress moves into the later portion of the video. Use a green emphasis on “Clean energy”, a dynamic text shadow during the light phase, and a bottom progress cue with sun icon, horizontal progress bar, and moon icon. Trigger an occasional solar glint after about 1100ms and then at random 4000–7000ms intervals at one of seven predefined points; the glint lasts about 1600ms.

The global Nav listens to a custom hero-progress event. While the hero is not scrolled, change the nav to a translucent black/white mode after progress passes about 0.35, make the logo and links readable against the dark video, and show the day/night state. When scrolled past 40px, use a near-white translucent background, backdrop blur, border, and dark text.

Solutions

Use a light-gray section with eyebrow “Solutions”, heading “Two paths. One clean future.”, and supporting copy explaining that Sunfield designs for single homes through property portfolios. Show two responsive image cards:

- Commercial — “Solar for the places you build.” — scalable systems for offices, retail, hospitality, warehousing, and mixed-use — button label “Explore commercial” to /commercial.

Use 4/3 image cards with rounded corners, subtle scale around 1.02 over approximately 700ms, green link hover, and keyboard focus rings. Before navigation save the current home scrollY and the solutions section to sessionStorage.

Benefits

Use a white section with heading “Why the smart move is the sun.”. Show four numbered benefits with thin divider lines and a hover line that expands from zero width:
1. Lower monthly energy costs. Offset the majority of the bill and lock in predictable rates.
2. Independence from the grid. Generate, store, and use power with outage and peak-rate resilience.
3. Long-term property value. Solar properties can resell at a measurable premium.
4. Cleaner air, quieter impact. Lifetime systems offset CO2 and quietly work in the background.

Use an IntersectionObserver at roughly 0.15 threshold. On entry, each item should animate from opacity 0 and translateY 12px to visible, with a 90ms stagger and approximately 700ms transition. A small hover translate and top-line reveal may be used.

Get Your Solar Estimate

Build a two-step form:

Step 1 — Your property:
- Property Type: required select with Residential — single family, Residential — multi-family, Commercial, Agricultural / farm.
- Property Location / ZIP: required text, maxLength 20.
- Average Monthly Electricity Bill: required number, minimum 0.
- Property Ownership: required select with I own the property, I rent, Business owner / operator, Property manager.
- Interested in battery storage?: required radio cards Yes — include a battery, Maybe — show me the numbers, No — solar only.
- Continue button validates these fields natively, changes the progress line to step 2, and scrolls the estimate section into view after approximately 40ms.

Step 2 — Contact:
- required Name;
- required Email;
- required Phone;
- required Financing Preference with Cash purchase, Solar loan, Lease / PPA, Not sure — show me options;
- optional Utility Bill Upload accepting PDF/JPG/JPEG/PNG and displaying the selected filename.
- Back button returns to Step 1.
- Submit button says “Get My Estimate” or “Submitting…” while loading.

Include hidden website_alt honeypot. On submit, URL-encode form fields and POST to the external Readdy endpoint only as a placeholder. Remove website_alt. If a file is selected, remove the file object and append utilityBill with the string “Uncollectable”; do not claim real file upload. Handle success, error, network error, spam text, form reset, step reset, and success message “Thanks — your estimate request is in. A specialist will reach out within one business day.”
