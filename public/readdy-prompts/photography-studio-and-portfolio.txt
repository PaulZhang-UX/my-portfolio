1. 设计基础 Prompt
============================================================

Create a minimal editorial photography portfolio for a photographer named Maren Voss, working between Berlin and Lisbon. The interface should feel like a contemporary photo exhibition and fashion magazine contact sheet: quiet, precise, human, and image-led. Keep the UI monochrome so the photography carries the color.

Use Inter Tight for display headings and Inter for body copy, labels, metadata, and navigation. Use compact modern sans-serif typography, small uppercase micro labels with approximately 0.12em letter spacing, large but restrained section headings, and concise editorial copy. Avoid decorative serif branding, heavy bold type, and generic agency dashboards.

Use a neutral theme system. In light mode use white #FFFFFF, warm surface #F5F5F2, divider #DEDEDC, text #0B0B0B, secondary text #555555, and muted text #898989. In dark mode use #090909, #121212, #292927, #F2F2EF, #A6A6A2, and #777773. Use square corners and one-pixel rules; reserve rounded geometry for a small scroll cue only.

Build a 1200px content system with a 64px sticky top navigation, generous 16px mobile and 40px desktop side gutters, and disciplined editorial spacing. Use four-column by two-row contact-sheet grids for work, 4:5 images for work cards, full-bleed studio imagery, and a spacious dark footer. Provide a light/dark toggle persisted in local storage and initialized from the user's system preference.

Use fashion campaigns, portraits, beauty, still life, lifestyle, hospitality, food, and behind-the-scenes studio imagery. Use object-cover in grids and object-contain in the full-screen detail viewer. Preserve focal positions supplied by the image data.

The motion language should reference photography and film: continuous image streams along a soft path, restrained proximity magnification, a camera-shutter transition between the hero and work archive, grid previews that unfold into larger frames, a filmstrip detail viewer, gentle editorial reveals, and subtle button/underline movement. Do not add neon, particles, bounce, or generic app animation. Respect prefers-reduced-motion by freezing image streams, removing transforms, and making content immediately accessible.
